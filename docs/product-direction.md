# Anchor: a journal for everyday life

Anchor helps people notice their lives, choose what matters, and revisit what
they learn. Overwhelm is one use case alongside good moments, ordinary days,
relationships, ideas, and personal change.

## The product loop

1. **Today:** begin with an intention, pause when useful, capture a moment, or
   reflect in the evening. Every route is optional.
2. **Journal:** find and revisit the actual words, feelings and actions recorded
   on a day. Search is local and includes notes, next steps and reflections.
3. **Review:** revisit a seven-day window, inspect the source entries, and write
   a reflection with an optional next step. The reflection becomes a dated note
   in today's journal, with the reviewed period included in its text.

## Implemented in this iteration

- Today / Journal / Review navigation on the three main screens.
- Begin / Pause / Reflect entry points to the existing rituals.
- A reusable composer with acknowledged native saving and retry behavior.
- Journal search across written fields, including dates and next steps.
- Seven-day review navigation, existing mood/sleep/habit summaries, verbatim
  excerpts, and links that expand the source day in Journal.
- Weekly reflection capture using the existing journal schema and export.

The review describes recorded data. Missing data is not zero. Excerpts are the
user's text; the app does not infer psychological traits or causal relationships.

## Remaining before the envisioned first release

- Native voice capture with clear permission, interruption and privacy behavior.
- First-use guidance, accessibility and real-device usability validation.
- Backup/recovery and account-sync testing across versions and devices.
- TestFlight recruitment and testing whether people return without prompting.
- Pricing and payment experiments after observing repeated product value.

Longer-term personal directions should connect to this loop rather than become
a separate task manager. Photos, advanced analysis and AI summaries need their
own evidence of value. This iteration is not a complete App Store release.
